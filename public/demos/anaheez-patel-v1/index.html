01 — About

Where luxury meets behaviour.

Anaheez Patel works at the intersection of luxury, branding and social psychology, with a close eye on culture and what it says about why people want what they want.

Her background spans the editorial world of Vogue India and Condé Nast India and an MBA at NYU Stern. This site brings that thinking, along with her advisory work and writing, into one considered home.
