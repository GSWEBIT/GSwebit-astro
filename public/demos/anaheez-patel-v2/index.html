Where luxury meets behaviour.

Anaheez Patel works at the intersection of luxury, branding and social psychology, with a close eye on culture and why people want what they want.

With a background in the editorial world of Vogue India and Condé Nast India and an MBA from NYU Stern, this site gives her advisory work and writing one considered home.
