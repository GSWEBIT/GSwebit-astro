    FAQ

    Good to know.

    Do I need experience for Power Yoga?

    No. Richa offers modifications so beginners and regular practitioners train together safely.

    Is prenatal yoga safe for me?

    That is what the intake and doctor clearance are for. Share your stage and any concerns and Richa will adapt the plan.

    When do I get my Zoom link?

    Instantly after payment, then again as a reminder before every class.

    Which payment methods work?

    UPI, debit and credit cards, and net banking.

    What if I miss a class?

    Rescheduling and recording policies are set by Richa. Sample answer for this preview.
